# Knowledge-base health review

Review a representative or complete collection as scope permits. Report recommendations before changing structure.

## Inventory

- Identify entry points, indexes, major domains, document types, ownership signals, and lifecycle metadata.
- Note the search and link capabilities of the environment.
- Sample recent, old, highly linked, unlinked, very large, and very small documents.
- Map important domains to candidate authoritative sources.

## Detect

### Duplicates

Find similar titles, repeated distinctive phrases, parallel summaries, and multiple pages answering the same question. Distinguish legitimate audience-specific views from competing sources.

### Contradictions

Search consequential claims and their opposites. Compare scope, dates, authority, and actual behavior. Do not flatten a historical change into a conflict.

### Staleness

Inspect temporary, current, planned, soon, versioned, and role-dependent language; dead projects; old screenshots; obsolete commands; unverified runbooks; and pages superseded without markers.

### Fragmentation and overload

Flag topics dispersed across disconnected small notes and documents combining unrelated audiences, tasks, authority, or lifecycles. Do not use file size alone as the decision.

### Discovery and links

Find orphaned pages, broken links, missing index entries, circular navigation, weak titles, missing aliases, inaccessible sources, and pages whose incoming links imply authority they do not have.

### Governance

Find domains with no authoritative home, unclear owners where ownership matters, conflicting taxonomies, duplicated metadata, and archives that appear current.
